Entity relationships

PostgreSQL is the production database. The diagram below shows the core relationships; every scan is the central aggregate for analysis artefacts.

Organization (1) ────< (N) OrganizationMember (N) >──── (1) User Organization (1) ────< (N) Project User (1) ───────────< (N) Project.created_by User (1) ───────────< (N) Scan.triggered_by Project (1) ────────< (N) ProjectArchive Project (1) ────────< (N) Scan Scan (1) ───────────< (N) ParsedDependency Scan (1) ───────────< (N) Vulnerability ──(N:1)──> ParsedDependency Scan (1) ──────────── (1) SBOM Scan (1) ──────────── (1) GraphMetric Scan (1) ───────────< (N) DependencyEdge Scan (1) ───────────< (N) RiskSignal ──(N:1)──> ParsedDependency Scan (1) ───────────< (N) LicenseRecord ──(N:1)──> ParsedDependency Scan (1) ──────────── (1) LicenseReport Scan (1) ───────────< (N) Recommendation ──(N:1)──> Vulnerability / ParsedDependency Scan (1) ───────────< (N) SecretFinding Scan (1) ───────────< (N) Report CVERecord (global, unique cve_id) · IntelSourceStatus (global source health)

Key constraints

  • Organization.name and (organization, name) for projects are unique.
  • (organization, user) membership is unique; roles are OWNER / ADMIN / MEMBER / VIEWER.
  • (scan, vuln_id, dependency, package_version) prevents duplicate advisory findings.
  • (scan, package_name, version) prevents duplicate license records.
  • (scan, source, target, edge_type) prevents duplicate graph edges.

Retention

Archives are retained for reproducibility and can be deleted through the maintenance Celery task. Reports are stored under media/reports/ with a SHA-256 checksum recorded on each Report.
